Welcome to Parrot Bay Motel and Hammock Shop

Parrot Bay Motel and Hammock Shop is a small, family-run motel on the beautiful island of Isla de Tuerte, Nicaragua. We are far from the large resorts, busy roads, and cruise ports. Our guests come for quiet beaches, clear water, and an uncomplicated place to stay.
